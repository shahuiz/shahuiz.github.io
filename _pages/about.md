I am currently a PhD candidate at Nanyang Technological University (NTU). I am supervised by Prof. [Jian Guo](https://guo.crypto.sg/) and funded by [Nanyang President's Graduate Scholarship](https://www.ntu.edu.sg/nie/admissions/graduate-education-admissions/scholarships-awards/nanyang-president's-graduate-scholarship-(npgs)). I received my BSc in Math from NTU in 2023 with [KK Pua Gold Medal](https://www.ntu.edu.sg/spms/about-us/awards/student-awards#kkpgm) and [Ng Kong Beng Best Project Prize](https://www.ntu.edu.sg/spms/about-us/awards/student-awards#ngkongbeng). I am an alum of [Tianjin Nankai High School](https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E5%A4%A9%E6%B4%A5%E5%B8%82%E5%8D%97%E5%BC%80%E4%B8%AD%E5%AD%A6).

My research interests lie in [quantum-safe cryptography](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Post-quantum_cryptography), with a focus on [symmetric-key algorithms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Symmetric-key_algorithm). I have specialized in the cryptanalysis of international cryptographic standards, evaluating their security in practical instantiations and against a range of attack scenarios in both classical and quantum settings. My work has set and sustained multiple records in the cryptanalysis of [AES](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Advanced_Encryption_Standard) and [SHA-2](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SHA-2). I am also interested in low-latency cipher design, hash-based and symmetric-key-based signatures, and privacy-preserving technologies.

Last time I checked, my [Erdős number](https://www.csauthors.net/distance/tianyu-zhang-004/paul-erdos) was 4.
